# Claims policy (fictional example)

- Never claim Kubernetes, Spark, Scala, CUDA or people management: target postings often ask for them; they are
  gaps, not skills.
- The ranking models were trained by Jordan; the A/B test that measured the 6.1% lift was run with the product team:
  say "measured in an online A/B test", not "designed the experiment platform".
- The retrieval prototype is a prototype: never call it a production system.
- The open-source work is one merged pull request: "contributed", never "maintainer".
- Keep every number exactly as written (2 million weekly users, 6.1%, 140 features, 38%, 12 A/B tests, 300 stores,
  18% to 13%).
- Do not mention work authorization in resumes, cover letters or messages.
